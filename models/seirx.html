SEIRx disease and vaccination behaviour¶

b_seirx_tc · tipbox.models.SEIRx

The physical state is susceptible \(S\), exposed \(E\), infectious \(I\), and vaccinator fraction \(x\). Recovered individuals are implicit in the total population \(N\):

\[\begin{split}dS &= [\mu N(1-x)-\beta SI/N-\mu S]dt+\eta\sigma_S dW_S,\\ dE &= [\beta SI/N-(\epsilon+\mu)E]dt+\eta\sigma_E dW_E,\\ dI &= [\epsilon E-(\gamma+\mu)I]dt+\eta\sigma_I dW_I,\\ dx &= \kappa x(1-x)[-\omega(t)+I+\delta(2x-1)]dt +\eta\sigma_x dW_x.\end{split}\]

Time is measured in weeks. The vaccination-risk forcing ramps from omega_min to omega_max over \(T/\text{param_end_multiplier}\) and then stays constant. The automatic null cap is \(\max(\omega_{\min},2\omega_c-\omega_{\max})\); omega_null overrides it. The disease-free behavioural marker is \(\omega_c=\delta\), unless an explicit omega_bifurcation is supplied.

The initial state is \((0,0,0,1)\). Each Euler–Maruyama update first floors all four coordinates at 1e-8, then clips \(x\) to \([0,1]\) and rescales \((S,E,I)\) if their sum exceeds \(N\). This retained numerical floor seeds tiny exposed and infectious populations even with noise=0; the implemented disease-free boundary is therefore approximate. Observations contain \((I,x)\); all four sampled physical coordinates remain in result metadata.

Default critical parameter: 50. Automatic markers follow the physical coefficients where supported; AMOC uses an explicitly calibrated marker.
