Rate-induced saddle-node normal form¶

r_saddle_node · tipbox.models.SaddleNode

\[\begin{split}dX &= [(X+\Lambda(t))^2-1]dt+\sqrt{2\eta}\,dW_t,\\ \Lambda(t)&=\frac{\Lambda_{\max}}2 \left[\tanh\left(\frac{\Lambda_{\max}\epsilon t}{2}\right)+1\right].\end{split}\]

The frozen equilibria translate as the forcing increases. Sufficiently rapid translation can drive a trajectory away from the stable branch without a local loss of frozen-system stability. The null scenario multiplies epsilon by null_rate_factor. The time origin is absolute; the main forcing change is near zero. Initial states are uniform on the configured interval.

Here noise is \(\eta\), a diffusion intensity. Its per-step scale is \(\sqrt{2\eta\,dt}\). The tipping marker is the first state crossing of tip_threshold. Scalar observations have one column in make_trajectory; the historical simulator retains its dummy second column.

For the deterministic tracking solution with lambda_max=3, the rate threshold is epsilon_c=4/3. The configured epsilon=1.25 is below it; positive paths at the default rate are noise-assisted. The figures explicitly use epsilon=1.4. The null rate is halved, so the batch compares different forcing histories. Neither protocol reproduces a matched-forcing experiment.
